# MoveMate AI

## A Personalised AI-Powered Relocation Planning Assistant for Australian Renters

---

## Project Overview

MoveMate AI is a capstone project developed as part of **PROF909 – IT Project**. The project aims to design and develop an AI-powered relocation planning assistant that helps Australian renters organise and manage the moving process through personalised planning, smart reminders, AI-generated checklists, and tailored recommendations.

The project follows an **Applied Build (MVP)** approach and will be developed during Part B of the capstone.

---

## Problem Statement

Moving house involves coordinating multiple tasks such as packing, utility transfers, address updates, inspections, bookings, and strict deadlines. Existing moving applications mainly rely on generic checklists that do not adapt to an individual's circumstances.

MoveMate AI addresses this gap by using Artificial Intelligence to generate personalised relocation plans based on each user's moving requirements.

---

## Research Evidence

The project is informed by current research demonstrating that:

- AI improves planning and decision-making for complex tasks.
- Personalised recommendations increase user satisfaction and planning effectiveness.
- Existing moving applications provide static checklists rather than AI-driven personalised planning.

The project proposal is supported by peer-reviewed research and industry reports from organisations including Deloitte, IBM, Microsoft, McKinsey, NIST, and the Australian Government.

---

## Proposed Solution

MoveMate AI will collect basic relocation information from users, including:

- New address
- Moving date
- Budget
- Moving type
- Personal preferences

The AI planning engine will generate:

- Personalised moving checklist
- Smart timeline
- AI reminders
- Packing suggestions
- Relocation plan

## Ethics, Privacy and Responsible AI

MoveMate AI follows responsible AI principles by:

- Protecting user privacy
- Collecting only essential information
- Promoting transparency in AI-generated recommendations
- Aligning with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)
